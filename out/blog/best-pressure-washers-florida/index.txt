<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<h3>How often should I pressure wash my driveway in Tampa Bay?</h3>
<p>Most Tampa Bay driveways need cleaning every 4-6 months due to our humidity and frequent rain creating ideal algae conditions. Properties with significant tree cover or pool splash-out may need monthly attention during peak growing season (April through October).</p>
<h3>Can I use any detergent with my pressure washer?</h3>
<p>Not all detergents work safely with pressure washers or in Florida's environment. Use only detergents specifically formulated for pressure washers, and choose biodegradable options when cleaning near storm drains or natural areas. Household cleaners can damage equipment seals and harm local waterways.</p>
<h3>What's the biggest mistake Tampa Bay homeowners make with pressure washing?</h3>
<p>Using too narrow a nozzle (0° or 15°) on concrete surfaces, which can permanently etch or damage the surface. Florida's sun-baked concrete is particularly susceptible to damage. Always start with a 25° nozzle and adjust as needed, maintaining 12-18 inches distance from the surface.</p>
<h3>Should I seal my driveway after pressure washing?</h3>
<p>Yes, especially in Florida's climate. A quality concrete sealer applied after thorough cleaning and complete drying extends the time between deep cleanings from 4-6 months to 8-12 months. The investment pays for itself through reduced maintenance and better appearance retention.</p>
<h3>Is it worth buying a gas pressure washer for a typical Tampa Bay home?</h3>
<p>For most single-family homes with standard driveways and pool decks, a quality electric unit like the Sun Joe SPX3000 provides sufficient power while being easier to maintain and store. Gas units make sense for properties over 3,000 sq ft of concrete surfaces or homes requiring frequent heavy-duty cleaning.</p>
