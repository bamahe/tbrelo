<h2>Why Tampa Bay Has a Pest Problem (And Why It's Not Going Away)</h2>
<p>Our subtropical climate creates bug paradise. Humidity hovers around 75% year-round, temperatures rarely dip below freezing, and we get enough rain to keep everything breeding. Add in dense vegetation, aging housing stock, and constant new construction disturbing established colonies, and you've got a recipe for pest pressure that never lets up.</p>
<p>The most common invaders I see affecting home sales and daily life:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Subterranean termites</strong> — cause $5+ billion in damage nationally, with Florida leading the charge</li>
<li><strong>American cockroaches</strong> (the big flying ones) — thrive in our humid conditions</li>
<li><strong>Mosquitoes</strong> — active 10+ months per year here</li>
<li><strong>Ants</strong> — fire ants, ghost ants, carpenter ants</li>
<li><strong>Spiders</strong> — including occasional black widows and brown recluses</li>
<li><strong>Fleas and ticks</strong> — thanks to year-round outdoor activity</li>
</ul>
<p>The key insight: reactive pest control doesn't work here. You need proactive, consistent treatment whether you see bugs or not.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<h3>How often should I have professional pest control service in Tampa Bay?</h3>
<p>Quarterly service is the minimum for effective pest control in Tampa Bay's climate. Monthly service during peak season (April-October) provides optimal protection, especially for mosquito control. Properties with persistent pest pressure or sensitive occupants may benefit from monthly year-round service.</p>
<h3>Do I need termite protection if my home is newer?</h3>
<p>Absolutely. Termites don't discriminate by home age, and newer construction often disturbs existing colonies. Florida building codes require termite pre-treatments for new construction, but this protection degrades over time. Most lenders require active termite bonds regardless of home age.</p>
<h3>Is professional pest control safe around pets and children?</h3>
<p>Modern pest control products are significantly safer than older formulations. Professional-grade products target pest biology specifically and break down quickly in the environment. However, keep pets and children away from treated areas until products dry (typically 2-4 hours), and always inform your technician about household pets.</p>
<h3>What's the difference between big national companies and local pest control services?</h3>
<p>National companies typically offer broader service guarantees, established protocols, and easier transfers if you move. Local companies often provide more personalized service, competitive pricing, and detailed knowledge of neighborhood-specific pest pressures. Both can be excellent choices depending on your priorities.</p>
<h3>Should I do my own pest control or hire professionals?</h3>
<p>DIY pest control works well for maintenance and minor issues, but Tampa Bay's pest pressure typically requires professional-grade products and application expertise. The most effective approach combines professional quarterly treatments with homeowner maintenance using quality retail products between visits.</p>
